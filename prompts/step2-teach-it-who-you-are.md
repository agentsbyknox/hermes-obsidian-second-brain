# Step 2: teach it who you are

Edit the notes in `10 Identity/` first. Then send:

```text
My Obsidian vault is my Second Brain. Read the notes in '10 Identity' and save the durable facts about me to your memory: who I am, how I like answers, and my rules. Don't change anything in the vault. Then list what you saved.
```

Built-in memory is small (a few thousand characters), so only facts needed in *every* session belong there. Everything else stays in the vault and is recalled on demand.
